A few things to know.

Which Mahjong style is this for?

Mahjong Calculator is built for American Mahjong, with regular and tournament scoring options.

Do I still need my Mahjong card?

Yes. Use your card to identify your hand and its base value. The app helps you calculate the score; it does not replace your Mahjong card.

Where can I download the app?

Mahjong Calculator is available for iPhone and iPad on the App Store. Visit the listing for current pricing and compatibility.
